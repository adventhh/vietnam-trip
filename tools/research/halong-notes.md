# Hanoi / Hạ Long research notes — 22–24 Sep 2026

## PLAN-AFFECTING FINDINGS

1. **Chả Cá Thăng Long is at 6B (and 2D) Đường Thành, not 19.** The Michelin Guide 2025 Bib Gourmand listing and the restaurant's own site both give 6B Đường Thành, Cửa Đông. The block has copycat chả cá shops, so the house number matters.
   - https://guide.michelin.com/my/en/ha-noi/ha-noi_2974158/restaurant/cha-ca-thang-long
   - https://chacathanglong.com.vn/en/home/

2. **Amanda's published Hanoi pick-up window is 07:45–08:30, not 07:30.** Their own itinerary page and the operator listing both state Old Quarter hotel collection at 07:45–08:30, arriving Tuần Châu 11:30–12:00. The 07:30 in the itinerary is ~15–60 min early. Amanda normally phones/messages the hotel the evening before with a firm time — confirm at check-in on 22 Sep.
   - https://amandacruise.com.vn/itineraries/
   - https://www.halongbaycruises.com.vn/tours/amanda-halong-cruise-2-days-1-night.html

3. **11:30 is arrival at the marina, not boarding.** Marina check-in is 11:30–12:00, then a tender out to the ship; boarding and cabin check-in is ~12:00, lunch is served underway. Budget the extra 30 min.
   - https://amandacruise.com.vn/itineraries/

4. **Amanda Day 1 includes Titop Island and Sung Sốt cave — both are stair climbs.** Titop is a ~400-step summit climb and Sung Sốt is a long stepped cave route. Given the "zero strenuous activity" priority, plan to skip both and stay on the sundeck; kayaking on Day 2 is also optional. Confirm with the cruise director that opting out is fine (it is, but say so early).
   - https://www.halongbaycruises.com.vn/tours/amanda-halong-cruise-2-days-1-night.html

5. **Day 2 disembark is ~11:00, not 11:30.** Cabin checkout 09:30, early lunch buffet 10:00, marina arrival 11:00. That is ~30 min of spare time in your favour on 24 Sep.
   - https://www.halongbaycruises.com.vn/tours/amanda-halong-cruise-2-days-1-night.html

6. **Taking the private car back is the right call.** Amanda's own return shuttle does not reach Hanoi hotels until 15:00–15:30 — that misses the 14:15 luggage drop and the 15:45 airport departure entirely. The private car (11:30–14:15) is what makes 24 Sep work; do not let the cruise desk switch you onto the group shuttle.
   - https://www.halongbaycruises.com.vn/tours/amanda-halong-cruise-2-days-1-night.html

7. **Tuần Châu is still the correct 2026 port for Amanda** — specifically "No. 12, Tuan Chau Marina". Do not let a driver take you to Hạ Long International Cruise Port at Bãi Cháy, which is a different terminal 8 km away on the mainland.
   - https://amandacruise.com.vn/itineraries/
   - https://cruiseinhalongbay.com/destination/tuan-chau-marina/

8. **The rest stop is Trạm dừng nghỉ V52 Hải Dương, Km52+500 on the Hanoi–Hải Phòng Expressway (CT.04)**, Bình Giang, Hải Dương (20.8578, 106.3326). It sells bánh đậu xanh (Rồng Vàng Minh Ngọc, Hòa An, Nguyên Hương), bánh gai Ninh Giang and ổi Thanh Hà, plus fast food, coffee and a convenience store. The other stop on the route, V23 at Km24+700 (Hưng Yên), comes too early to be the break. **Bánh giò is a Hanoi street snack and is not reliably sold at highway rest stops** — buy that in the Old Quarter on 22 Sep instead of expecting it on 23 Sep.
   - https://ivexe.vn/diem-dung-chan-cao-toc-ha-noi-hai-phong/
   - https://trungtamcuuho119.vn/tram-dung-nghi-cao-toc-ha-noi-hai-phong/

9. **Maison Marou is 91A Thợ Nhuộm, not 91.** Minor, but Grab pins on the wrong number.
   - https://www.corner.inc/place/272698

10. **Blackbird Coffee is not "nearby" Maison Marou.** The Chân Cầm branch is ~1.0 km north near St Joseph's Cathedral (12 min walk / 5 min Grab). It is an alternative to Marou, not a same-hour second stop in a 75-minute window.
    - https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g293924-d14140860-Reviews-Blackbird_Coffee_Chan_Cam-Hanoi.html

11. **Bánh Mì Phố Huế is not near Thợ Nhuộm either.** It is 118 Phố Huế, Hai Bà Trưng — 1.5 km south-east of Marou and the opposite direction from Nội Bài. If you want bánh mì on 24 Sep, either accept a 6–8 min Grab each way or substitute Bánh Mì 25 on 22 Sep near the hotel.
    - https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g293924-d7813402-Reviews-Banh_My_Pho_Hue-Hanoi.html

12. **Chả Cá Lã Vọng closes 14:00–17:00 and shuts at 21:00.** A 19:30 dinner is fine but is close to the practical last seating. It is also roughly double Thăng Long's price with mixed reviews (3.6–4.3 on Tripadvisor) — Thăng Long is the better pick on both value and walk distance.
    - https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g293924-d33246669-Reviews-Cha_Ca_La_Vong-Hanoi.html

13. **The Alchemist is on the 2nd floor of 19 Nguyễn Quang Bích with no street-level signage.** Small room; message the Facebook page ahead for a 21:00 table.
    - https://www.facebook.com/thealchemistcocktailbar/

14. **Tranquil closes 22:30 and is a quiet reading cafe, not a bar** — fine for 21:00–22:30, but Tranquil and The Alchemist are 100 m apart, so you can decide on the night rather than pre-committing.
    - https://www.instagram.com/tranquilbookscoffee/

## PRACTICAL

- **Weekend pedestrian zone: no effect on your dates.** Hanoi's Hoàn Kiếm walking streets run 19:00 Friday to midnight Sunday only. 22 Sep is a Tuesday and 24 Sep a Thursday, so Grab and cars reach Đường Thành, Cầu Gỗ and Hàng Gai normally on both days, and there are no road closures around the hotel.
  - https://evivatour.com/hanoi-walking-street-when-where-to-go/
  - https://en.vietnamplus.vn/pedestrian-spaces-in-hanoi-resume-operation/223757.vnp

- **VietJet domestic cut-offs: bag drop closes 60 min before departure, check-in counters close 40 min before.** For VJ521 at 18:45 that is **17:45 for checked bags** and 18:05 for counter check-in. Arriving ~16:30 leaves a comfortable ~75 min of margin. Check in online (open from 24 h to 1 h before) and you only need the bag-drop counter.
  - https://www.vietjetair.com/en/pages/how-to-check-in-1685510691899
  - https://www.vietjetair.com/en/FAQ/flight-procedures-1619722426262

- **Grab at Nội Bài T1:** T1 handles domestic. Departures are on the **2nd floor** — say "ga đi T1, tầng 2" or the driver defaults to the ground-level arrivals kerb and you carry bags up. Select "Noi Bai International Airport (T1 Domestic)" as the destination in the app, not the generic airport pin, or you may be routed to T2 (~600 m away). Old Quarter → T1 via Nhật Tân bridge is 40–50 min and roughly 280,000–400,000 VND.
  - https://www.grab.com/global/airport-rides/noi-bai-international-airport/
  - https://vinwonders.com/en/wonderpedia/news/noi-bai-international-airport-ultimate-guide-for-arrivals-and-departures/

- **22 Sep evening is fully walkable.** Hotel (55 Đường Thành) → Chả Cá Thăng Long 250 m, → Tranquil 250 m, → The Alchemist 300 m, → Cafe Phố Cổ 430 m, → Chả Cá Lã Vọng 600 m. No Grab needed all evening.

- **Bags on 23 Sep:** boarding at Tuần Châu is by tender from the marina, so soft-sided luggage is easier down the gangway than hard shells. Amanda includes the return transfer, cabin, four meals, activities, entrance fees, Wi-Fi and an English-speaking cruise director; drinks, spa and private-car upgrades are extra.

- **What to bring on the cruise:** passport (checked at the port), swimwear and a light layer for the sundeck at night, reef shoes if you do want the beach, motion-sickness tablets, and cash or a card for the drinks bill, which is settled at checkout on the morning of the 24th.

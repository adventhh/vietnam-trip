# Hoi An research notes — 24–26 Sep 2026

## PLAN-AFFECTING FINDINGS

### 0. They land on the biggest lantern night of the year — Thu 24 Sep 2026 is the Full Moon Lantern Festival AND Mid-Autumn eve

The festival runs on the **14th of each lunar month**, and the 2026 dates are 27 Jul, 26 Aug, **24 Sep**, 23 Oct, 22 Nov, 22 Dec. **24 September is the eve of Tết Trung Thu** and is described as one of the two largest lantern nights of 2026 — children's lantern parades and lion dances through the Old Town on top of the normal festival.
<https://www.nghevilla.com/en/blog/hoi-an-lantern-festival-2026-calendar-dates> · <https://hoiandaytrip.com/hoi-an-lantern-festival/> · <https://madmonkeyhostels.com/blogs/hoi-an-lantern-festival-guide/>

What this changes:
- **Electric street lighting in the Ancient Town is switched off roughly 18:00–22:00**, leaving only lanterns and moonlight. The 21:30–23:00 walk therefore catches the **last ~30 minutes** of the lantern-only window. Move it earlier if at all possible — drop bags and go straight out, do not shower first. Everything after 22:00 is the ordinary lit town with the crowd still in it.
- **This is the night for the lantern boat, not Friday.** The plan puts the boat at Fri 18:00; the festival boat night is Thursday. If they can be at the An Hội pier by ~21:45 they get the festival version. Otherwise keep the Friday 18:00 booking — Friday is Mid-Autumn day itself and still busy and lit, just without the lights-off effect.
- **Crowds and traffic.** Expect the ĐT607 run and the last few hundred metres to Lantana to be slower than the 20:30–21:20 estimate, and the riverfront to be at maximum density. Confirm the airport transfer in advance rather than gambling on a Grab into a congested town.
- **Fri 25 Sep is Tết Trung Thu itself** — lion dances and parades in the Old Town during the day. It is **not** a Vietnamese public holiday, so tailors, cafés and the ticket booths trade normally, but restaurants will be full: book the 19:30 Morning Glory table.
- Photography: lights-off means very low light. Bring the iPhones' night mode expectations down — handheld video of the boats and lanterns works better than stills.

### 1. Tailoring — a 21:30 Thursday measurement does not work. Friday 08:00 is the only realistic slot.

- **Closing times.** Yaly Couture trades daily **08:00–21:30**. Bebe Tailor's published hours vary by source between **08:00–20:00** and **08:00–21:30**; neither shop keeps different Thursday hours.
  <https://yaly-couture-exclusive.wheree.com/> · <https://goodmorning-hoian.com/en/best-tailors-in-hoi-an/> · <https://bebetailor.com/>
- **Why 21:30 fails.** Landing 20:05 → Grab 20:30–21:20 → walk from Lantana. Bebe 1 is ~1.2 km east of the hotel (15 min on foot); Yaly Exclusive ~800 m. Arrival at the door is 21:35–21:50 at best — at or past the shutter, and the cutting floor closes before the showroom does. A measurement needs 45–60 min per person for fabric and style choice, which no shop will start at closing.
- **Friday 08:00 is fine** — both chains open at 08:00, and Yaly Signature (47 Nguyễn Thái Học) is already on the planned 09:00–11:30 shopping street.
- **Turnaround reality.** Hoi An express work is **24–48 h for simple pieces**; casual clothes are normally quoted **3–4 days**; suits **4–5 days**. Two days is the accepted absolute minimum for anything with fittings.
  <https://vietnamjourneysgroup.com/hoi-an-express-tailoring-experience-get-custom-clothes-in-just-24-48-hours-2337.html> · <https://vietnamdiscovery.com/hoi-an/top-lists/tailoring-in-hoi-an/> · <https://adongsilk.com/how-long-to-make-suit-hoi-an/>
- **Per garment, against a Sat 09:30 departure (≈25 h from a Fri 08:00 measure):**
  - **Linen trousers** — doable. Simplest block, one fitting, hem adjusted same evening.
  - **Resort / camp-collar shirts** — doable, and the safest bet of the three. Ask for a machine-finished collar, not hand-rolled.
  - **Silk (dress, blouse, silk trousers)** — **do not order.** Silk is cut slower, marks if unpicked, and needs a second correction the schedule has no room for. Yaly's own guidance is a 3-day minimum on tailored garments.
- **Fitting schedule that actually fits the itinerary:**
  | Time | Action |
  |---|---|
  | Fri 08:00–09:00 | Measure + fabric at Yaly Signature (47 Nguyễn Thái Học) or Bebe 1. State "24-hour express" at the start, before choosing fabric. |
  | Fri 16:30–17:45 | First fitting — slot it between Faifo Coffee and the 18:00 boat; both shops are ≤5 min from Trần Phú. |
  | Fri 20:30–21:15 | Collect, after dinner and before the 21:30 shutter. **Treat this as the real deadline.** |
  | Sat 08:00–09:00 | Fallback collection only. Do not plan on it — it leaves no margin before the 09:30 Grab. |
- **Cap the order at 2–3 simple pieces per person.** Get the collection time written on the receipt and pay a deposit only. Ask at measuring what international shipping to Singapore costs, as the fallback if anything is not ready by Friday night.
- **Which shop:** Yaly Signature for convenience (it is on the Friday morning route). Bebe 1 if the priority is speed — it is the branch with tailors on site rather than work sent out, but it costs a 1.2 km walk each way, three times.

### 2. Japanese Covered Bridge — open, no works in 2026
Restoration ran 2022–2024 and the bridge **reopened in August 2024**. Fully open and scaffolding-free. Walking across is free; the Ancient Town stub is only taken to enter the shrine interior.
<https://www.asiantrails.travel/latest-news/hoi-an-bridge-reopen/> · <https://hoianheritage.net/en/specialised-exchanges/research-exchange/japanese-covered-bridge-in-hoi-an-renovations-1.html>

### 3. Ancient Town ticket — 120,000 VND, on-site only
120,000 VND for foreigners (80,000 VND Vietnamese), five tear-off stubs covering five of the 22 heritage sites. Sold at **11 booths** around the old town — including one just behind the Japanese Bridge (closest to Lantana), one at the An Hội bridge foot, one on Hai Bà Trưng. Booths sell **07:00–21:30** in summer, to 21:00 in winter. **It cannot be bought online**; anything sold online is a reseller. Needed for Tấn Ký, the assembly halls and the bridge interior — not for walking the streets, where enforcement is loose.
<https://hoianit.com/hoi-an-entrance-ticket/> · <https://hoiandaytrip.com/hoi-an-old-town-ticket/> · <https://hoabinhtourist.com/en/blog/hoi-an-ancient-town-ticket>

### 4. Lantern boat — priced per boat, not per person
Syndicate-fixed: **150,000–170,000 VND for 1–3 people, 200,000–220,000 VND for 4–5**. Two people pay the 1–3 rate — roughly S$8–9 total, not each. Ride is ~20 min between the An Hội and Cẩm Nam bridges. Floating lanterns are extra, 5,000–15,000 VND each. **Buy at the official counter at the start of Bạch Đằng or at the An Hội bridge foot**, not from the touts working the promenade. The 18:00 slot is right: boats run ~17:30–22:00 and the sky still has colour at 18:00 in late September.
<https://hoiandaytrip.com/hoi-an-lantern-boat-ride/> · <https://dananghoian.net/en/explore/lantern-boat-floating-on-hoi-ans-hoai-river-price-guide/> · <https://www.yourvietnamtravel.com/hoi-an-lantern-boat-ride>

### 5. Thursday 21:30–23:00 snacks — the plan needs redirecting to the night market
Almost nothing sit-down is still serving at 22:00. **Cao lầu** houses (Trung Bắc, Thanh) shut in the evening. **Bánh xoài and thịt xiên nướng are street-cart food and are not sold in restaurants** — the reliable source is the **Hoi An Night Market on Nguyễn Hoàng** (An Hội island, 17:00–23:00, stalls thin out after 21:00 but food stalls run latest). It is a 7 min walk from Lantana over the An Hội bridge, and it is lantern-lit, so it doubles as the first photo stop.
The one late cao lầu fallback is **Bánh Mì Phượng**, open to 21:30, but that is the far east end of town.
<https://hoianit.com/nguyen-hoang-night-market-hoi-an-a-must-visit-place/> · <https://hoiandaytrip.com/mango-cake-hoi-an/> · <https://lasiestaresorts.com/banh-mi-hoi-an.html>

### 6. Friday timing conflicts to fix
- **Madam Khanh closes 19:00 and Bánh Mì Phượng 21:30** — the 13:00–14:00 slot is safe for both. Madam Khanh is the better pick for two people who want to sit down: no queue. Phượng means 15–30 min standing.
- **White Rose (533 Hai Bà Trưng) is ~900 m north-west of Trần Phú**, a 10–12 min walk from Faifo Coffee, and the walk is on an ordinary road, not old town. Combining it with the Faifo rooftop in the 14:30–17:30 block means two round trips. Either drop it, or do White Rose first (14:30) and Faifo second (16:00–17:30), which also puts you at the rooftop for the good light.
- **Tấn Ký Old House and the assembly halls close around 17:30**, so they must stay in the 09:00–11:30 block.
- **Faifo Coffee's rooftop terrace is small and fills from ~15:30.** On a Friday it runs 08:00–21:30. If it is full, The Chef (166 Trần Phú) has the same view one block west.
- The 09:00–11:30 shopping block overlaps the **09:00–11:00 pedestrian-only window**, which is ideal — Trần Phú and Nguyễn Thái Học are motorbike-free then.

## PRACTICAL

**Grab at Da Nang airport (Thu 20:05 arrival)**
- Pick-up is **not** at the taxi rank. Clear customs, exit the international arrivals hall and cross to the outer edge of the forecourt — **Lane 1 / Lane 2**, signed in English as the ride-hailing zone. Drag the pin to that exact point in the app before confirming, or the driver will circle.
- Fare Da Nang airport → Hoi An is well above the in-city rates; there is a **30,000 VND airport pick-up surcharge paid to the driver in cash**, on top of the app fare. Evening and rain both trigger ~1.5× surge, and 20:30 is peak arrivals.
- The hotel's own transfer is **USD 18 for a 4-seater (+USD 10 after 22:00)**. At a 20:30 pick-up the surcharge does not apply, and against a surging evening GrabCar plus the airport fee this is close to a wash with none of the finding-the-lane friction — worth pre-booking for the arrival leg specifically. Use Grab for the Sat 09:30 return, when there is no surge.
  <https://www.grab.com/global/airport-rides/da-nang-international-airport/> · <https://www.dananghotelguide.com/da-nang-grab-guide.html> · <https://hoianitinerary.com/hoian-airport-guide>

**Ancient Town vehicle-free hours**
- Pedestrian and non-motorised only, **daily ~09:00–11:00 and ~15:00–21:30** (sources vary: some give 09:00–11:30 and 15:00–21:00; winter ends 21:00, summer 21:30). Outside those windows motorbikes and cars use Trần Phú and Nguyễn Thái Học.
  <https://goodmorning-hoian.com/en/hoi-an-ancient-town/> · <https://hoiandaytrip.com/is-hoi-an-walkable/>

**Grab drop-off for Lantana**
- Lantana is at **9 Thoại Ngọc Hầu**, on the **north bank ~350 m west of the Japanese Bridge and outside the vehicle-free core** — a Grab can drive to the door at any hour, including during the 15:00–21:30 pedestrian window. The hotel also has free on-site parking, so there is no drop-and-walk.
- Coming from the old town at night, the nearest a car can reach the core is the Nguyễn Thị Minh Khai / Phan Chu Trinh perimeter; from the riverfront that is a 5–8 min walk out to the pick-up point. Set the Grab pin on the hotel, not on the Ancient Town, when heading back.

**Ancient Town at night**
- The streets themselves never close and are free to walk. What closes is the **ticketed** layer: booths stop selling at 21:30 (summer), heritage houses and assembly halls at ~17:30, the bridge shrine ~21:00.
- Lanterns are lit from dusk; the riverfront and the An Hội bridge are the busiest between 18:30 and 20:30. **On Thu 24 Sep the Ancient Town's electric lighting is off ~18:00–22:00 for the Full Moon Lantern Festival** — see finding 0.

**Two practical notes for this pair**
- Everything on both days is inside a **1.2 km east–west strip** and completely flat — no strenuous element anywhere in the plan.
- Cash: Madam Khanh, the night market carts, the boat counter and the cao lầu shops are cash-only or cash-preferred. The tailors take cards. Withdraw once on Thursday night.

## PLAN-AFFECTING FINDINGS

### Blocking

1. **Moana Sapa closes at 17:00 — the 17:15–18:30 slot is entirely after closing.**
   Moana operates 07:00–17:00 daily, and ticket sales reportedly stop around 16:30. The plan arrives
   15 minutes after the gate shuts. Fix: swap Moana and Absinthe on Monday — Moana 14:45–16:15
   (which is also the recommended 15:00–17:00 golden-hour window for portraits), then Absinthe from
   16:30, which is open until 23:00 and gets better as the lights come on. A few listings claim 19:00
   closing, so it is worth a phone check that morning, but do not plan on it.
   Sources: https://kampatour.com/moana-sapa · https://sapanomad.com/moana-sapa/ ·
   https://www.flysapa.com/en/post/moana-sapa-review-tips

2. **Fansipan: the 20 Sep assumption is correct, and that is precisely the risk.**
   Sun World Fansipan Legend shuts the cable car AND both funiculars (Mường Hoa and summit) for annual
   maintenance **21–27 Sep 2026**, reopening 28 Sep. So Sun 20 Sep is genuinely the last operating day —
   a Sunday, and the last chance for a week. Expect the heaviest queues of the month and no fallback if
   the weather closes in. Buy the combo ticket online the night before and be at Sun Plaza for the 07:30
   first departure. Re-verify the dates about a week out; maintenance windows shift with weather.
   Source: https://vecaptreosapa.com/lich-bao-tri-cap-treo-fansipan-2026-tam-dung-tu-21-09-den-27-09/

3. **Sunday is Fansipan's shortest operating day: 07:30–14:30.**
   (Sat 07:30–15:30, Mon–Thu 08:00–14:30, Fri 08:00–15:30.) The 07:30–11:30 plan fits, but it depends on
   the 07:30 first departure — which only runs at weekends. Getting the 05:30 bus arrival, bag drop and
   breakfast done in time for a 07:30 boarding is the tightest sequence of the trip.
   Source: https://sapanomad.com/fansipan-cable-car-ticket-review/

### Timing and ordering

4. **HK Buslines does not send the limousine to the hotel door.**
   A shuttle collects from hotels in the Sapa centre zone and delivers to the HK office at 697 Điện Biên
   Phủ, where the coach actually departs. So "12:30 pick-up at Pistachio" is the shuttle, and the real
   departure is later. Treat 12:30 as a hard deadline, be in the lobby by 12:15, and reconfirm the pick-up
   with HK the day before. Sources: https://sapanomad.com/hk-limousine-hanoi-sapa/ ·
   https://joytime.vn/en/product/3702-hanoi-sapa-shuttle-service-by-hk-buslines.html

5. **THE MIST SAPA is on Thác Bạc, not Hoàng Liên — and that makes it the right Tuesday pick.**
   11A Thác Bạc is the same street as Pistachio, a few minutes on foot. Viettrekking is the Hoàng Liên
   cliffside deck. With a 12:30 pick-up, the walkable one wins: no taxi, no return leg. The Mist is closed
   Mondays (irrelevant for a Tuesday) and opens 07:30. Note one source reports a ~17:00 close, which does
   not affect a late-morning visit. Sources: https://sapanomad.com/the-mist-cafe-sapa/ ·
   https://coool.cafe/cafe/the-mist-sapa-sa-pa

6. **Viettrekking's famous cloud sea is an October–April, before-08:00 phenomenon.**
   A 10:45 visit in late September gets the green Mường Hoa valley and possibly the funicular gliding
   through it — a good view, but not the floating-cloud shot the photos sell. If that shot is the point,
   it has to be a sunrise stop, not a check-out stop. Source: https://sapanomad.com/viettrekking-sapa-cafe/

7. **Cốn Sủi Ông Há closes at 14:00 and is not walkable from Sun Plaza.**
   Hours 04:00–14:00; the 11:45–13:00 lunch is the last safe window. It sits at 591 Điện Biên Phủ, roughly
   1.5 km downhill/north-east of the centre — take a taxi, do not walk it after a morning on the mountain.
   35,000–40,000 VND a bowl, cash. Source: https://phoconsui.com/

8. **Sapa Stone Church interior is shut by the time the plan arrives, and Sunday Mass is at 19:00.**
   Visiting hours are Sun 09:00–18:00; the 19:30–21:30 slot means the floodlit exterior and the square
   only. That is the photogenic version anyway — just do not plan on going inside.
   Source: https://www.yourvietnamtravel.com/sapa-stone-church

9. **Red Dao herbal bath in a 60-minute slot is tight.** A proper bath is 20–30 minutes in the tub plus
   changing, and going straight from a hot herbal soak into a walk is unpleasant. Dao's Care runs
   08:00–23:30, so it can move later; if the day slips, this is the correct thing to drop, as the plan
   already allows. Source: https://sapanomad.com/red-dao-herbal-bath-embrace-deep-healing-experience/

10. **Absinthe afternoon tea has no published price.** The bar runs 11:00–23:00 daily (no children after
    18:00), so the slot is fine, but the tea must be booked by phone or email —
    +84 214 362 9999 ext. 6426, minhanh.do@accor.com. Source:
    https://www.hoteldelacoupole.com/dining/absinthe/

### Confirmed as planned (no change needed)

11. **Sunday night market is real.** The N1 street market runs Fri/Sat/Sun, ~16:00–22:00 with stalls often
    to midnight, peak 20:00–22:00. The 19:30–21:30 slot is exactly right. Caveat: this is the modern night
    market, not the historic "love market" — no courtship ceremony.
    Source: https://sapanomad.com/night-market-sapa/

12. **Pistachio pool: 06:30–11:30 and 13:00–19:00.** The Tue 08:45–10:15 swim is inside the morning
    window; the midday closure at 11:30 is the thing to know.
    Source: https://pistachiohotel.com/wellness/infinity-pool

13. **Cat Cat: 150,000 VND adult entry, 06:00–18:00, rainbow slide 40,000 VND per ride.** Tickets at the
    gate only, **cash only** — no card, no online. The 09:45 arrival beats the 09:30-onward tour-group
    wave only just; earlier is better. Sources: https://asiamystika.com/blog/cat-cat-village-vietnam ·
    https://sapanomad.com/rainbow-slide-sapa/

14. **Alpine Coaster Bản Mòng is ~3.5 km SE of the square, not 5 km.** Hours reported as 07:00–18:30 or
    09:00–18:00 (sources disagree); 200,000–250,000 VND adult. Closes in heavy rain or fog — check the
    same morning. Sources: https://sapanomad.com/alpine-coaster-sapa/ ·
    https://www.klook.com/en-US/activity/43346-sapa-mong-village-alpine-coaster-experience/

15. **Quán Ô Quý Hồ is in town at 08 Thạch Sơn**, not out at the Ô Quý Hồ pass — a short taxi from the Cat
    Cat exit. Open 08:00–21:00, 0888 029 119. Note the spelling *Ô Quý Hồ* when showing a driver.
    Source: https://oquyhorestaurant.com/

16. **Of the two hotpot options, Hotpot Center (71 Xuân Viên) chains better into the evening** — it
    overlooks Sapa Lake, two minutes from the lake path and walkable to the night market. A Phủ
    (15 Fansipan, 09:00–23:00, 0868 159 900) is the better-known kitchen but sits the wrong side of town
    for a 19:30 market move. Either way, salmon and sturgeon are priced per kg — agree the fish weight
    before they cook it. Hotpot Center's menu is largely Vietnamese-only; staff use translation apps.

17. **The unnamed "phở gà near the central square" has a name: Phở Gà Hoàng Ngân**, immediately beside the
    Stone Church, breakfast only, closes when the broth runs out. The 06:15–07:15 slot works if they are
    open that early on a Sunday. Source:
    https://travelsapa.com.vn/chi-tiet-du-lich/quan-pho-ga-ngon-o-sapa-2166-1488.html

## PRACTICAL

**Grab and taxis.** Grab and Be both operate in Sapa, but coverage is thinner than in Hanoi and drops off
outside the centre — expect waits, and expect the Alpine Coaster and Moana areas to be hard to get a car
back from. Have the hotel arrange the Alpine Coaster and Moana legs, or ask the driver to wait. Most local
Sapa taxis have no working meter: **agree a fixed fare in VND before getting in.** Licensed cars have a
roof sign and the company name on both front doors.
Phone numbers: Sapa Xanh 0203 63 63 63 · Phan Xi Păng 0203 62 62 62 · Mai Linh 0214 3 76 76 76 ·
Hoàng Hợp 02143 843 843 · Vân Ngọc 02143 758 758.
Sources: https://thingstodoinsapa.com/transportation/taxi-rideshare/ ·
https://mytour.vn/en/blog/bai-viet/sapa-taxi-hotline-directory-detailed-phone-numbers.html

**Getting back up from Cat Cat.** The descent is easy; the climb back is the strenuous part to avoid.
From the bottom exit: xe ôm 50,000–60,000 VND per person, or a car 80,000–120,000 VND. Inside the site,
an electric buggy from Gate 2 down to the village centre is 10,000 VND per person per trip. Two people on
xe ôm is the fast option; a car is the sane one if it is raining.
Source: https://passportandstamps.com/sapa-cat-cat-village-guide/

**Cash vs card.** Carry more cash than feels necessary. Cash-only or effectively cash-only: Cat Cat gate
tickets, the night market, Phở Gà Hoàng Ngân, Cốn Sủi Ông Há, xe ôm, and most non-metered taxis. Card
works reliably at the hotels (Pistachio, de la Coupole), Absinthe, Sun World ticket counters and the
chain cafés. Draw cash in Hanoi or at an ATM in the town centre on arrival — do not rely on finding one
near Cat Cat or Bản Mòng.

**Weather, late September.** Mornings 16–19 °C, afternoons 22–25 °C — a light jacket is genuinely needed
in the mornings and at the summit. September is still inside the typhoon window; remnants can bring 2–3
days of heavy rain to northern Vietnam, and September is one of the wettest months in Sapa (~315 mm).
Rain closes the Alpine Coaster and fogs out both Fansipan and every view café. Upside: the terraces are in
golden harvest colour. Sources: https://asiamystika.com/blog/sapa-in-september ·
https://www.agatetravel.com/vietnam-weather-in-september.html

**Bag drop on arrival.** The whole Sunday plan rests on Pistachio holding bags from 05:30 against a 14:00
check-in. Confirm this by email before travelling.

**Coordinates.** Where Photon returned only a street or town, `approx` is `true` in `sapa.json` and the
coordinate is the street centroid — good enough to show a driver alongside the `vi` field, not good enough
for turn-by-turn. Affected: Pistachio, The Mist, Cốn Sủi Ông Há, HK Buslines office, A Phủ, Hotpot Center,
Cat Cat, Dao's Care, Sapa Sky View, Viettrekking, the night market, and the two bakeries. Everything else
is an exact venue match.
